# Business Decision Support

Investment decision support system for analyzing business opportunities and providing data-driven investment recommendations.

## Workflow Decision Tree

### Investment Analysis
Use "ROI Analysis" or "Company Profiling" sections below

### Risk Assessment
Use "Risk Analysis" workflow

### Strategic Planning
Use "Strategic Analysis" workflow

### Market Opportunity Evaluation
Use "Market Analysis" workflow

## ROI Analysis

### Financial Metrics Calculation
When evaluating investment returns, analyze these key metrics:
- **Net Present Value (NPV)**: Calculate discounted cash flows
- **Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**: Determine project profitability
- **Payback Period**: Assess time to recover investment
- **Return on Investment (ROI)**: Calculate efficiency ratio

### Value Assessment Framework
1. **Market Size Analysis**: TAM, SAM, SOM calculation
2. **Competitive Advantage**: Sustainable differentiation factors
3. **Financial Projections**: Revenue, cost, and profit forecasts
4. **Risk-Adjusted Returns**: Incorporate uncertainty factors

## Risk Analysis

### Risk Categories
- **Market Risk**: Competition, demand fluctuations, regulatory changes
- **Financial Risk**: Cash flow volatility, funding requirements
- **Operational Risk**: Execution challenges, scalability issues
- **Technology Risk**: Technical feasibility, obsolescence

### Risk Mitigation Strategies
1. **Diversification**: Portfolio risk management
2. **Hedging**: Financial risk protection mechanisms
3. **Contingency Planning**: Alternative scenarios and responses
4. **Insurance**: Risk transfer strategies

## Company Profiling

### Comprehensive Analysis
1. **Business Model Analysis**: Revenue streams, cost structure, unit economics
2. **Financial Health**: Balance sheet analysis, cash flow patterns, debt ratios
3. **Market Position**: Competitive landscape, market share, growth trends
4. **Management Team**: Experience, track record, leadership quality

### Due Diligence Process
- **Financial Documentation**: Review audited statements, tax returns
- **Legal Compliance**: Regulatory filings, contracts, IP protection
- **Operational Review**: Supply chain, customer relationships, quality controls
- **Market Validation**: Customer interviews, market research, competitive analysis

## Strategic Analysis

### SWOT Analysis Framework
1. **Strengths**: Internal capabilities, competitive advantages
2. **Weaknesses**: Internal limitations, improvement areas
3. **Opportunities**: External factors, market trends
4. **Threats**: Competitive pressures, market risks

### Strategic Recommendations
- **Growth Strategy**: Market expansion, product diversification
- **Competitive Strategy**: Differentiation, cost leadership, focus
- **Financial Strategy**: Capital structure, investment allocation
- **Operational Strategy**: Process optimization, technology adoption

## Market Analysis

### Market Research Methodology
1. **Market Sizing**: Top-down and bottom-up approaches
2. **Competitive Landscape**: Market share analysis, positioning strategies
3. **Customer Analysis**: Segmentation, needs assessment, behavior patterns
4. **Trend Analysis**: Industry evolution, technology adoption, regulatory changes

### Investment Theses Development
- **Market Opportunity**: Size, growth rate, fragmentation
- **Value Proposition**: Unique benefits, competitive differentiation
- **Business Model**: Revenue generation, scalability, sustainability
- **Exit Strategy**: M&A potential, IPO readiness, secondary markets

## Implementation Guidelines

### Data Collection
- **Primary Research**: Customer interviews, expert consultations
- **Secondary Research**: Industry reports, company filings, market data
- **Financial Modeling**: Excel models, scenario analysis
- **Benchmarking**: Industry standards, competitor analysis

### Analysis Tools
- **Financial Calculations**: NPV, IRR, sensitivity analysis
- **Risk Assessment**: Probability analysis, Monte Carlo simulation
- **Market Analysis**: Segmentation, positioning, competitive mapping
- **Strategic Frameworks**: SWOT, Porter's Five Forces, Blue Ocean

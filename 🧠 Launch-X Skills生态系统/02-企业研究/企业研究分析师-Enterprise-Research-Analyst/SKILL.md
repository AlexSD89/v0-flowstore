# Enterprise Research Analyst

Comprehensive enterprise research analyst for conducting due diligence, industry analysis, and competitive intelligence.

## Workflow Decision Tree

### Company Due Diligence
Use "Company Analysis" workflow below

### Industry Research
Use "Industry Analysis" workflow

### Competitive Intelligence
Use "Competitor Analysis" workflow

### Investment Research Reports
Use "Report Generation" workflow

## Company Analysis

### Financial Health Assessment
1. **Balance Sheet Analysis**: Asset quality, debt structure, equity position
2. **Income Statement Review**: Revenue trends, profitability, expense structure
3. **Cash Flow Analysis**: Operating cash, investment cash, financing activities
4. **Financial Ratios**: Liquidity, solvency, efficiency, profitability metrics

### Business Model Evaluation
- **Revenue Streams**: Primary and secondary income sources
- **Cost Structure**: Fixed vs. variable costs, unit economics
- **Customer Base**: Concentration, retention, acquisition costs
- **Scalability**: Growth capacity, operational leverage

### Management Assessment
- **Executive Team**: Experience, track record, leadership quality
- **Corporate Governance**: Board composition, transparency, accountability
- **Organizational Structure**: Efficiency, communication, decision-making
- **Company Culture**: Innovation, risk appetite, employee engagement

## Industry Analysis

### Market Structure
1. **Market Size**: Total addressable market, serviceable market, obtainable market
2. **Growth Trends**: Historical growth, future projections, growth drivers
3. **Competitive Landscape**: Market share concentration, barriers to entry, competitive dynamics
4. **Regulatory Environment**: Compliance requirements, regulatory changes, licensing needs

### Value Chain Analysis
- **Supplier Power**: Bargaining position, switching costs, concentration
- **Buyer Power**: Customer leverage, price sensitivity, switching barriers
- **Substitute Threats**: Alternative solutions, switching costs, price-performance
- **Industry Profitability**: Average margins, return on capital, capital intensity

### Technology Assessment
- **Current Technologies**: Industry standards, technology adoption rates
- **Emerging Trends**: Disruptive technologies, innovation pathways
- **Digital Transformation**: Automation opportunities, digital readiness
- **R&D Investment**: Innovation capacity, patent portfolios, research spending

## Competitor Analysis

### Competitive Positioning
1. **Market Leaders**: Market share, strategies, strengths, weaknesses
2. **Emerging Competitors**: Growth trajectories, innovative approaches, threat levels
3. **Niche Players**: Specialization areas, competitive advantages, market focus
4. **New Entrants**: Potential entrants, barriers to entry, threat assessment

### Strategic Analysis
- **Business Models**: Revenue models, cost structures, value propositions
- **Growth Strategies**: Expansion plans, acquisition history, innovation focus
- **Competitive Advantages**: Sustainable differentiation, cost leadership, customer relationships
- **Market Positioning**: Brand perception, price positioning, quality positioning

### Performance Benchmarking
- **Financial Metrics**: Revenue growth, profitability, efficiency ratios
- **Operational Metrics**: Customer satisfaction, market share, brand awareness
- **Innovation Metrics**: R&D spending, patent filings, product launches
- **Market Penetration**: Geographic coverage, customer segments, channel presence

## Report Generation

### Investment Research Reports
1. **Executive Summary**: Key findings, investment recommendation, risk highlights
2. **Company Overview**: Business description, history, products/services, markets
3. **Financial Analysis**: Performance trends, valuation, financial health assessment
4. **Investment Thesis**: Investment rationale, growth drivers, risk factors, exit strategy

### Due Diligence Reports
- **Business Due Diligence**: Operations, management, market position, growth prospects
- **Financial Due Diligence**: Historical performance, financial projections, valuation analysis
- **Legal Due Diligence**: Corporate structure, contracts, compliance, intellectual property
- **Tax Due Diligence**: Tax structure, compliance status, optimization opportunities

### Industry Research Reports
- **Market Overview**: Size, growth, trends, drivers, challenges
- **Competitive Analysis**: Key players, market shares, strategies, positioning
- **Technology Trends**: Current state, emerging technologies, adoption patterns
- **Future Outlook**: Growth projections, opportunity analysis, risk assessment

## Research Methodology

### Data Sources
- **Primary Research**: Company interviews, customer surveys, expert consultations
- **Secondary Research**: Industry reports, analyst research, financial databases
- **Public Information**: SEC filings, company websites, press releases, news articles
- **Proprietary Data**: Industry databases, market research, competitive intelligence

### Analytical Frameworks
- **SWOT Analysis**: Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats assessment
- **Porter's Five Forces**: Industry attractiveness, competitive intensity analysis
- **Financial Modeling**: Discounted cash flow, comparable company analysis, precedent transactions
- **Scenario Analysis**: Base case, optimistic, pessimistic scenario development

### Validation Techniques
- **Data Verification**: Cross-checking multiple sources, consistency checks
- **Expert Review**: Subject matter expert validation, peer review process
- **Sensitivity Analysis**: Impact assessment of key assumptions and variables
- **Reality Testing**: Model validation against historical performance and industry benchmarks
